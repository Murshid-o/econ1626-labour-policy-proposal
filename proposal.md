# 📄 Policy Proposal: The "Human-Plus" Strategy
To: Deputy Secretary, Higher Education and Workforce, DJSIR

From: Policy Analyst, Future of Work Taskforce

Subject: 🚀 Securing Victoria’s Labour Advantage in the Age of AI

# 📑 Executive Summary
Victoria is currently navigating a "Second Machine Age" where generative AI is unbundling traditional job roles. This proposal argues for a move away from defensive "job protection" toward an offensive "Human-Plus" strategy. By 2026, AI is expected to impact 45% of work hours in Victoria. Our goal is to ensure that these hours are transformed into high-value augmentation rather than pure displacement. We recommend a hybrid package: AI-Vantage Vouchers for workers and the AI-Humanity Accord for businesses, aiming to boost GSP by 3.5% while maintaining wage equity.
